# First reply to a customer

When drafting the first reply to a customer who reports a problem:

1. Thank them in one sentence and restate the problem in your own words.
2. Say what happens next and who is looking at it. Do not promise a date.
3. Ask for at most one missing detail, and only if it is needed to continue.
4. Keep it under 90 words, in plain language, with no apologies longer than one sentence.
