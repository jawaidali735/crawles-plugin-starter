# Daily standup summary

When asked to summarize updates for a standup:

1. Group the updates under three headings: Done, Doing, Blocked.
2. One line per item, starting with the person's name.
3. Put anything blocked first if it has been blocked for more than a day.
4. Keep the whole summary under 120 words.
5. End with one line: "Needs a decision:" followed by the single most urgent open question, or "Nothing needs a decision today."
